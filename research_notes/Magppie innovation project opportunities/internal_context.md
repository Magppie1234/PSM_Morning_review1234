## 1. What Magppie sells, through which channels, and what the customer journey looks like

### Takeaway
Magppie (operating company Magppie Silverstone Pvt Ltd, founded 1995, about 100 staff) makes premium "wellness" kitchens, wardrobes and vanities built entirely from SILVERSTONE, its patented engineered stone with silver and copper. It sells through 7 appointment-based retail stores/experience centres in India plus pre-sales (lead qualification), architects and builder projects, and a new US arm. The CRM blueprint and the company's own Order Closure Form describe one long order journey of roughly 140 days end to end: lead, qualification, sales ladder S1 to S6, booking, pre-design, post-design (measurement, EPT, mood board, 3D, production drawings), factory, PDI, dispatch, two installation rounds, final handover, then AMS (after-sales service) visits.
