# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React + Vite frontend and Express backend. This keeps the standalone operational dashboard fast to run locally and ready for a CRM integration.

## Users

Senior PSM leaders running daily morning reviews and the PSMs whose lead pipelines are discussed.

## Product Purpose

PSM Morning Review turns yesterday's lead activity into a practical leadership workspace for identifying risks, deciding next actions, and improving lead-to-booking conversion.

## Positioning

The dashboard combines PSM comparison, risk detection, decision-ready lead context, and a conversion funnel in one daily operating view.

## Operating Context

Used in daily PSM meetings, normally with the reporting period set to Yesterday. Leaders review the team, then drill into leads requiring action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Filters: reporting period, PSM, city, product, and lead source.
- KPI, risk, PSM performance, decision queue, and funnel data come through the backend API.
- The initial build uses explicitly marked demo data; Zoho CRM mapping is a later integration step.
- This app remains independent from existing MAGPPIE dashboards.

## Brand Commitments

The supplied PSM Morning Review mockup is the binding design reference: clean white workspace, deep navy type, precise indigo accents, compact executive data density, and restrained semantic risk colors.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with actions and risks, not passive reporting.
2. Make PSM comparison quick enough for a morning meeting.
3. Every summary number should be traceable to a lead list.
4. Keep daily metrics, pipeline progress, and business value visible together.
